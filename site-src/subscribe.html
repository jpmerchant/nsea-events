Pick your group and tap a button. New and changed events update in your calendar automatically.

By practice group

Everything else

Subscribe to one of: Everything, or one or more groups. Group calendars already include the all-group events, so combining Everything with a group shows duplicates.

Help

Apple Calendar (iPhone, iPad, Mac)

Tap "Apple / Outlook" and confirm Subscribe. To change how often it refreshes: Settings > Calendar > Accounts > Subscribed Calendars.

Google Calendar

Tap "Google" and confirm the add. Google refreshes subscribed calendars on its own schedule, usually within a day. On the phone app, the calendar appears after you add it on the web version once.

Outlook or something else

Use "Outlook web" or "Copy link", then add a calendar "from internet" or "by URL" and paste the link.
